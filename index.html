Find the right bin on the Davis campus

Most trash cans on campus only take landfill. Scan the code on one, say what you're holding, and get walking directions to the nearest compost, recycling or battery bin.

How it works

Codes go on trash cans that have no compost or recycling bin next to them.

  1. 01

    Scan the code on the can

    The code knows where that can is, so there's no location prompt.

  2. 02

    Pick the bin you need

    Compost, recycling, or a multi-bin for batteries and small electronics. Not sure? Search for the item.

  3. 03

    Walk there

    Open walking directions in Google Maps to the closest station from the campus sustainability map.
